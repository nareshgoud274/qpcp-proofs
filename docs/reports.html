        What a report can and cannot do

        • A report marks the lemma "under review" on the map until it is resolved.
        • A confirmed report never rewrites a frozen statement. The lemma is retired and stays visible; a corrected version enters as a new lemma.
        • A proof that Lean has checked cannot be "wrong". What can be wrong is the statement: it may say something other than the mathematics intends. Those are the reports that matter most.
