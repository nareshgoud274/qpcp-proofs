The conjecture

  
Are there fixed integers q, k ≥ 2 and constants 0 ≤ a < b ≤ 1 for which the following problem is QMA-hard?

  
Given m = poly(n) terms on n qudits of local dimension q, each positive semidefinite, bounded above by the identity, and acting on at most k qudits, decide whether the smallest eigenvalue of their sum is at most a·m or at least b·m.

  
Formulation as recorded in the QIQCOP Zoo entry on the quantum PCP conjecture. Check the primary sources before relying on it.
