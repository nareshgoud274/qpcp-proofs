      Barrier desk

      Some approaches to the conjecture are known not to work. Before effort goes into a mandate that aims beyond the foundations, anyone can test it against these barriers and flag it. A flag is advice from a contributor. It is not a verdict of the checker.

      Why some programs are closed

      Contributors can state lemmas about the definitions the project already has. They cannot yet add definitions. The closed programs need new ones, such as quantum verification and reductions, and a wrong definition would quietly spoil everything built on it. Those are waiting for a specialist in Hamiltonian complexity to write and review them. Closing a program stops new proposals only: its existing steps stay open to prove or refute, and its results stay in the record.

      That is also the honest limit of this project today: the open programs build and explore the ground the conjecture stands on. They do not yet reach the conjecture itself.
