What support pays for

    What support does not buy

    • No sponsor can change a statement, a definition, or what the checker accepts.
    • No sponsor's proofs are merged on different terms from anyone else's.
    • Sponsorship is not a claim that the conjecture will be resolved, here or anywhere.
